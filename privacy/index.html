PlainSet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

In short: PlainSet has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share your data. Your training log lives on your own devices.

What PlainSet stores, and where

  • Workout data (exercises, sets, weights, reps, notes, routines, body weight entries, settings): stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • iCloud backup (optional, off by default): if you turn it on in Settings, your workout data is copied to your own private iCloud (CloudKit) database under your Apple Account. The developer cannot see or access it. You can delete that copy any time with "Delete my iCloud copy" in Settings.
  • Apple Health (optional): with your permission PlainSet reads body weight and heart rate, and saves finished workouts, duration, heart rate and your effort score to Apple Health. This data is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Location (optional): used only to remember where your gym is and, if you allow it, to offer starting a workout when you arrive. Location is processed on your device and never leaves it.
  • Camera (optional): used only when you photograph a gym machine so it can be identified on your device. Photos are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Motion (Apple Watch, optional): used to count reps during a set. It stays on your watch.
  • Notifications (optional): local reminders such as the end of a rest timer or your free trial ending, scheduled on your device.
  • Apple Intelligence features: "Ask your log" and "Log by text" run on your device with Apple's on-device model, or with simple built-in rules when it is not available.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details or your name. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent or share personal data. Files you export or share (CSV, JSON, .plainset programs, recap cards) go only where you choose to send them.

Tracking and analytics

PlainSet does not track you across apps or websites and contains no analytics or advertising code.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your devices until you delete it. Deleting the app removes the local data. To remove an iCloud copy, use "Delete my iCloud copy" in Settings. You can export everything as CSV or JSON at any time from Settings, even if you stop subscribing.

Children

PlainSet is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
