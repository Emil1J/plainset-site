PlainSet Support

Need help? Email bake.pious1c@icloud.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and PlainSet build number (Settings > About). We read every message.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. PlainSet has no accounts. Your log stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

How do I back up my data or move to a new phone? Turn on "Back up with iCloud" in Settings. On the new phone, sign in with the same Apple Account, open PlainSet and choose "Restore from iCloud". You can also export everything as CSV or JSON from Settings at any time.

How do I bring my history from Strong or Hevy? Export a CSV from that app, then in PlainSet choose "Import from Strong/Hevy" on the first screen, or Settings > Data.

How does the free trial work? The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions and you will not be charged. PlainSet can send one reminder two days before the trial ends.

How do I restore my purchase? Tap "Restore purchases" on the subscription screen or in Settings, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

How do I cancel my subscription? Settings > your name > Subscriptions > PlainSet. Cancelling never deletes your data, and you can still export it.

The Lock Screen Live Activity doesn't show. In iOS Settings > PlainSet, make sure Live Activities are allowed, then start a workout and lock the phone.

My Apple Watch doesn't show my workout. Open PlainSet on both devices once. The Watch app works on its own and syncs when the two can talk to each other.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app to remove local data. Use "Delete my iCloud copy" in Settings to remove an iCloud backup.
